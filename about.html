About Metallurgeon

Specialized welding and engineering solutions for heavy industry.

Who We Are

Metallurgeon specializes in the import of high-quality electrodes and welding wires used for repairing heavy mechanical machinery. Our solutions are particularly focused on machinery used in cement factories, where equipment operates under demanding conditions.

We provide specialized welding solutions for repairing components such as hammers, rollers, sleeves, and tables. Our objective is to help industries restore damaged components, extend equipment service life, and reduce unnecessary machinery replacement.
